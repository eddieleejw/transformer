## Tiny Shakespeare

The `data/tinyshakespeare/input.txt` dataset was obtained from Andrej Karpathy's [char-rnn repository](https://github.com/karpathy/char-rnn/tree/master/data/tinyshakespeare).

The upstream repository declares the MIT license in its README, but does not provide a separate license file.

The dataset consists of excerpts from the works of William Shakespeare, which are in the public domain.
